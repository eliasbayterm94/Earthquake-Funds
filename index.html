The earthquake hit the farms we buy from.

On August 10, 2026, a magnitude 7.4 earthquake with its epicenter in San José del Palmar, Chocó, struck western and central Colombia. The Eje Cafetero is inside the affected area, and so are the producing families Forest works with.

This is not a cause Forest picked. It is the region Forest buys from.
