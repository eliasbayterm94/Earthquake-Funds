The earthquake hit our communities. We will rebuild them together.

On August 10, 2026, a magnitude 7.4 earthquake with its epicenter in San José del Palmar, Chocó, struck western and central Colombia. The Eje Cafetero is inside the affected area, and so are the producing families Forest works with.

This is not a cause Forest picked. It is the region Forest buys from.

Transparency Read before you give

What you can hold us to.

Reporting

Every week we post the running account on Forest Coffee's social channels: what came in, what Forest added through the match, and what was bought or delivered with it. Weekly, for as long as the campaign is open and until the funds are spent.

Taxes

Donations are received by Forest Coffee LLC, a trading company — not a registered charity. That means your contribution is not tax-deductible. We are saying it here rather than in fine print, because the alternative is you finding out at filing season.
